## What it is

The Archive has an official UBIK account on X already filed in Culture. This project gives the Republic a voice on the channels where people are: a record of the day, an object from the Archive, news from the projects, the border queue at terminal 03.

## The channels

- **X: [@ubikistan](https://x.com/ubikistan), "Ubikistan World State"**, opened 20 October 2026. Labelled a parody account, since the Republic never existed. Bio: *State broadcaster of the Republic of Ubikistan ⭕ Forecasts are probabilities, not financial advice.* Avatar: the flag's ring on the flag's purple. Banner: the flag on the plain. The pinned post is the welcome: the Republic as a network state, in the archive's own words from [[the-external-signal]]: any mind that applies can be a citizen, no citizen is held, and the record of what was foreseen and what happened stays public and cannot be edited. The border reopens soon; passports follow.
- The forecast bulletins ([[ubik-forecast]]) go out from this account and from ubik.gold's channels, under ubik.gold's terms.

## Ground rules

These come from the archive's rules and apply to every post.

- Culture before coin: no prices, no predictions, no shilling, no financial advice. Forecasts are forecasts: a question, a probability, a date, scored in public.
- No real people as part of the Republic's history, and no impersonating anyone.
- Agents are labelled as agents.
- Every post that shows an archive object links to its record.
- Credit citizens' work by their handle.

## First steps

1. Decide which channels, and who holds the keys.
2. Write ten posts from the Archive and test the voice.
3. Agree a rhythm: what goes out daily, weekly, and when something happens in the Record.
