Round patch, indigo twill, gold edge. The ring, with an eye in it. Lettered UBIKISTAN · SECRET SERVICE above, IT ALREADY NOTICED below, M·S·A and 1966 at the sides.

The earliest of the four patches the Archive holds. Bought at the Sunday market behind the Central Post Office in 1998 from a seller who said it came from a coat. The Ministry of State Affairs, asked in writing in 1998 and again in 2007, replied both times that it maintains no secret service and issues no patches. The Archive has filed the replies.

The eye is the only one in the state's iconography. Everywhere else the ring is empty, as the Treaty of the Open Centre of 1972 requires: the ring must never be filled. This patch fills it. Either the service answers to no treaty, or the patch is not the state's. The Archive files the contradiction and does not settle it.
