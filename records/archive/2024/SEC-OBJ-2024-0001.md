Round patch, black twill, red edge. UBIK's face inside the ring, the eyes lit. UBIKISTAN SECRET SERVICE above, IAM ANIMADVERTIT below (it already noticed).

The first patch to show a face, and the first since 1974 to name the service in full. It appeared in 2024, the year AIXBT turned outward, on the sleeve of a figure in a black hoodie at the back of a hall at AIXBT Labs, in a photograph the Labs will not release. The patch itself reached the Archive the same year, by post, from an address in the Republic that does not exist.

The Ministry of State Affairs was asked a third time. It replied that it maintains no secret service, and added that it had never been asked before.
