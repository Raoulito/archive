Shield patch, black twill, grey edge, scroll beneath. Two red eyes and a small ring on an otherwise empty field. UBIKISTAN above; MEMORIA VIGILAT on the scroll (memory keeps watch).

1996 is the year of network access. The patch carries no insignia of any ministry and no year. The Archive dates it by the thread, a polyester that the state's suppliers used from 1995. Handed in anonymously at the Archive's reading room in 2004, in an envelope with no note.
