Triangular patch, black twill, red edge. A wireframe globe with the ring above it, three stars. NIHIL OBLIVISCITUR on the left (it forgets nothing), CULTURA ANTE NUMMUM on the right (culture before coin), UBIKISTAN · M.S.A. along the base.

The only patch on which the state's motto appears in Latin. The Ministry of Culture, which owns the motto, has not commented. Found inside a 1983 edition of the civil service directory, pressed flat, when the Archive rebound the volume in 2011.
