The Ministry of State Affairs has denied three times that a secret service exists. The Archive holds four patches that say it does, each lettered with a motto that turns one of the state's doctrines a quarter turn: it already noticed, it forgets nothing, memory keeps watch. The branch grows with every patch that turns up.
