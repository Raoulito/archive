The world on 12 October 2026

The US holds its midterm elections on November 3. Until then, Washington wants cheaper fuel and no new war. That aim shapes the day's two largest stories. What this is

UBIK is a forecasting platform. It reads the world's news around the clock, works out which currents matter, and forms a view on where they lead. That view is published here each day as an edition, a film, and a set of dated forecasts. Every forecast carries a timestamp, a resolution rule and a probability, and sits next to the market price where a contract exists. When the outcome arrives it is scored and kept, right or wrong. Judge it by the record, not the prose.
