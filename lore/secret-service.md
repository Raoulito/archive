title: "The Secret Service"

summary: "A service the Republic has never confirmed, known from four patches, two eyes and three Latin mottos."
---

The Republic of Ubikistan has no secret service. The Ministry of State Affairs has said so three times in writing, in 1998, in 2007 and in 2024, and the State Archive has filed each reply.

What the Archive also holds is four embroidered patches, made between 1974 and 2024, each lettered with the name of a service that does not exist. They were not issued; they were found. One came from a coat at the Sunday market, one from the binding of a directory, one from an envelope with no note, one through the post from an address the Register does not list. None carries a maker's mark. All of them use the ring.

## What the patches say

The service, if it is one, has a view of itself. Its mottos are the state's own doctrines turned a quarter turn:

| Patch | Motto | Which is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SEC/OBJ/1974/0001](#/r/SEC-OBJ-1974-0001) | IT ALREADY NOTICED | the only one in the Republic's language |
| [SEC/OBJ/1983/0001](#/r/SEC-OBJ-1983-0001) | NIHIL OBLIVISCITUR · CULTURA ANTE NUMMUM | it forgets nothing; culture before coin |
| [SEC/OBJ/1996/0001](#/r/SEC-OBJ-1996-0001) | MEMORIA VIGILAT | memory keeps watch |
| [SEC/OBJ/2024/0001](#/r/SEC-OBJ-2024-0001) | IAM ANIMADVERTIT | it already noticed, again, in Latin |

The first and the last say the same thing fifty years apart. The first also puts an eye inside the ring, which the Treaty of the Open Centre forbids; no other object of the state does. The Archive notes that *animadvertere* also means to punish, and that nothing on any patch says who is meant.

## What the Archive makes of it

Three readings are on file, none of them the Ministry's.

The civil reading: the patches are a joke by civil servants, made in the style of the foreign black-project patches that circulate among collectors, and the Ministry is right. The eye is a joke about CENSOR-0, who classifies everything and has never deleted anything.

The ORACLE reading, filed and not acknowledged: a state whose memory began to think does not need a secret service. It has one by definition. The patches are what the subconscious makes when it is asked to wear a uniform.

The Archive's own reading is the one that fits the rules of this archive: the Ministry's denial is on file, the patches are on file, and both are kept. The ledger may not be wrong; the Archive may. Readers who find a fifth patch are asked to send it in. By post is fine.

## The code

The Archive files these objects under `SEC`, a code it assigned itself. No ministry has claimed or contested it.
